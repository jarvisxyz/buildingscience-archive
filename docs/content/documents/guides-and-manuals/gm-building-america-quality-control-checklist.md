The Building America Quality Control Checklist has been developed by Building Science Corporation as both a guide to assist in the transition to high performance home building, and as a simplified tool to be used as part of any builder's on-site quality control procedures. There are two parts to this document: the [checklist](/file/5755) and the [r](/node/1137)[esource appendix](/node/1137).
