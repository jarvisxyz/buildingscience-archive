    * **Access Your Dashboard:** Click on **"My Account"** (found in the top navigation).
    * **Go to Your Files:** Click the **"Files"** tab on your account page.
    * **Save the File:** Click on the file name - your download will start immediately. We recommend saving the file to your device immediately, as some mobile browsers do not "save" PDFs automatically.
