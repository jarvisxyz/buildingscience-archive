BuildingScience.com Corporation regularly conducts workshops and seminars that cover both fundamental and advanced building science topics. We are frequently invited to present our [research](http://buildingscience.com/service/research-development-and-demonstration) in academic and professional conferences across the country. For recent and upcoming seminars and workshops by the BuildingScience.com team, visit our [Events and Training](https://buildingscience.com/upcoming-events-and-training) page.
BuildingScience.com also creates customized workshops, classes, and individualized training materials related to building science and high performance design. Topics include retrofitting historic buildings, mitigating risk in commercial and residential construction, energy efficient design, and strategies for below-grade water control.
